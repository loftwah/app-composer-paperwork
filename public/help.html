Finish the form here.

Open a PDF or try the fictional garden form. Select fills the PDF’s own fields. Pen draws anywhere. Text and Tick fill printed boxes, including scans. Circle draws a clean ring, and Signature stamps a transparent PNG from your device.

The tool you choose stays active. After one text item, tick, circle or stamp, click again for the next one—no toolbar trip. Choose Select, press V, or press Escape to stop.

Move and correct

Choose Select, then tap an addition. Its outline hugs the visible text, tick, ring, ink or signature. Drag it to move; drag its lower-right handle to resize (circles, ticks and signatures keep their proportions). Select text to edit its words, size and wrap width; the outline follows the rendered words rather than the wrap box. The colour and size controls restyle the selected tick, circle or ink. Delete selected removes your addition. Eraser removes your ink strokes. Undo and Redo restore your last changes.

To clear a native form field, focus it and choose Clear field beside the tool options (or in More). On a phone, use Select to scroll the document. Pen uses one finger to draw; two fingers change zoom when you lift them. Fit resets to page width; a manual zoom is kept when you change pages.

Your signature

Choose Signature to open a floating picker over the page; the document does not move. Add a transparent PNG signature or initials and look at the real preview. Small, Medium and Large set the starting size for new stamps. Pick a stamp and the picker closes so you can click or press-drag-release on any page; the Signature control keeps the chosen image visible, and you can keep stamping without reopening it. On a touch screen, a full preview lifts above your finger while the exact footprint stays on the page. Select a placed stamp to move or proportionally resize it. Removing a stamp from the picker does not remove copies already on the page. The picker holds several signatures for this tab, and images stay in browser memory only; they are never uploaded or saved to storage. You can also draw a signature with Pen.

Document properties

Open More, then Document properties to inspect and edit the PDF’s title, author, subject and keywords. Technical details shows creator, producer, dates, custom properties and readable XMP values. Apply changes adds one undoable edit; Cancel or Escape leaves the document unchanged. Save PDF or Print includes your applied properties, and reopening lets you edit them again.

Changed properties replace the document-level XMP packet to prevent conflicting values in different PDF readers. Clear properties also clears creator, producer, dates and custom document properties. This does not redact page content, remove attachments or clear metadata elsewhere in the PDF.

Save and print

The state beside the filename always says where you are: Ready, Changes not downloaded, Creating PDF, PDF downloaded, PDF ready to print, or Couldn’t finish—edits safe. Save PDF downloads a new completed file, preserving the original page and editable form values. It does not save a working copy in the browser. You can continue editing here; later changes need another download.

Print prepares the same completed bytes and asks the browser to open its print dialog. If no dialog appears, use Open completed PDF to print and that viewer’s Print action. Paperwork can confirm the PDF was prepared, not that a physical printer produced a page.

Reopening an exported copy lets you correct its native fields. Added ink, text, ticks and images become ordinary page content after export. They can be corrected individually while this editor session is still open.

Keyboard

V Select · P Pen · H Highlight · E Eraser · T Text · K Tick · C Circle · S Signature. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo; Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z redo; Ctrl/Cmd+S save; Ctrl/Cmd+P print. Tab reaches fields and additions. Arrow keys move a selected addition; Shift moves ten points. Plus/minus resize; Delete removes. Enter edits selected text. Focus the page and press Enter to place text, a tick or an imported signature with the active tool.

Compatibility

Standard PDFs up to 30 MB and 100 pages are supported, including ordinary AcroForm text, multiline, checkbox, radio, dropdown and list fields. XFA, password-protected and already certificate-signed PDFs are refused. Some special boxes require Text or Tick. Paperwork uses a bundled Latin font; a character it cannot save produces an error and keeps your work intact. Original content is preserved without converting whole pages to screenshots.

Privacy

Your documents and signature images stay in browser memory. There is no upload, server processing, analytics, account, document database or automatic browser storage. Closing or reloading clears unsaved work. Save your completed PDF before leaving. Ordinary app scripts, fonts and PDF rendering resources load from this site. Embedded PDF actions and scripts are never run; document actions are removed from completed exports.

Why Paperwork?

A form should not require printing, handwriting and scanning just to add a few answers. Paperwork combines the everyday steps in one editor: fill the fields, write on the page, add a visual signature and save the finished PDF.
